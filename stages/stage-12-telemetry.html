Stage 12 • Human-in-the-Loop

Telemetry & Operator Overlays

Real-time augmented telemetry for remote operators. Projects planned paths onto camera frames via GPU encoding, publishes interactive RViz marker arrays, and streams live Foxglove dashboards.

Detailed Sequential Sub-Steps

Step 12.1
Rate: 15 Hz Frame: camera_optical_link

Planned Path Camera Reprojection

path_overlay_node transforms /plan from map to camera_optical_link, projectPoints into pixel coordinates, and ships lightweight geometry over ZeroMQ.

Step 12.2
Rate: 30 FPS Frame: Jetson GPU

Jetson GPU Hardware H.265 Streaming

jetson_path_overlay.py receives projected path polygons, draws augmented green ribbon directly on live frame, and hardware-encodes to H.265 via nvv4l2h265enc.

Step 12.3
Rate: Continuous Frame: map / odom

3D RViz & Foxglove Studio Dashboard

Aggregates point clouds, bounding boxes, predicted trajectories, and costmaps into web-accessible dashboard.
