Stage 05 • Geometry & Filtering

3D LiDAR Preprocessing

Filters raw PointCloud2 in base_footprint, separates road surface via RANSAC plane + local elevation grid, clusters obstacle points into bounding boxes, and projects 2D LaserScan.

Detailed Sequential Sub-Steps

Step 5.1
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: base_footprint

Range & Ego Self-Box Culling

Discards points < 0.5m (sensor housing) and > 50.0m; crops robot chassis box [-0.45, 2.0, -0.9, 0.9, -0.3, 1.55] in base_footprint.

Step 5.2
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: base_footprint

Dual RANSAC Plane + Height Grid Ground Removal

RANSAC fits road plane (tilt near 0°, z near 0). Points within cell_height of lowest grid point are ground; prevents curbs becoming walls.

Step 5.3
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: base_footprint

2D Projected Obstacle Clustering

Projects non-ground obstacle points into 2D (x, y) to collapse vertical LiDAR rings, then clusters via KD-tree graph components.

Step 5.4
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: base_footprint

Radial LaserScan Flattening

Finds minimum range per bearing bin across vertical field of view to create synthetic 2D LaserScan for AMCL and 2D costmaps.
