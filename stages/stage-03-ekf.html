Stage 03 • State Estimation

Local Odometry Fusion (EKF)

High-frequency Continuous Extended Kalman Filter fusing wheel encoder linear velocity with IMU yaw rate to produce smooth, drift-resistant odom -> base_footprint transform.

Detailed Sequential Sub-Steps

Step 3.1
Rate: 100 Hz Frame: imu_link

Gyro Conditioning & Bias Nulling

gyro_conditioner measures zero-motion bias during initial standstill and dynamically rejects thermal gyro drift spikes.

Step 3.2
Rate: 30 Hz Frame: odom -> base_footprint

EKF State Prediction & Measurement Update

15-state EKF [x, y, z, roll, pitch, yaw, vx, vy, vz, vroll, vpitch, vyaw, ax, ay, az] integrates process model and fuses twist observation.

Step 3.3
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: odom

Covariance Explosion Watchdog

ekf_watchdog_node continuously audits P matrix trace; triggers emergency stop if covariance exceeds safety thresholds or TF publication halts.
