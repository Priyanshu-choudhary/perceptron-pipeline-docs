Stage 11 • Physical Execution

Actuator Output & Hardware

Translates high-level Twist commands into physical motor torques. stm32_bridge_node packs serial TinyFrames; onboard STM32 executes closed-loop PID velocity control across 4 skid-steer wheels.

Detailed Sequential Sub-Steps

Step 11.1
Rate: 20 Hz Frame: base_link

Command Velocity Framing

stm32_bridge_node converts vx (m/s) and wz (rad/s) into 0xA5 0x01 TinyFrames payload with XOR checksum.

Step 11.2
Rate: Hardware Loop Frame: STM32 Firmware

Communication Watchdog Timer

ECU hardware watchdog shuts off H-bridge PWM drives if no valid /cmd_vel frame is received within 300 ms.

Step 11.3
Rate: 100 Hz Frame: Chassis

Differential Kinematics & Skid Scrubbing

Calculates individual left and right track speeds: v_left = vx - (wz * track_width / 2); v_right = vx + (wz * track_width / 2).

Step 11.4
Rate: 100 Hz Frame: Motor Driver

Closed-Loop Motor PID Current Regulation

STM32 microcontroller executes 100 Hz PID loop against encoder feedback, driving high-current MOSFET H-bridges.
