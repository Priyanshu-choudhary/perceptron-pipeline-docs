Stage 11 • Vehicle Kinematics & Execution

Actuator Output & Vehicle Control

Translates velocity commands (/cmd_vel) into physical vehicle execution across dual backends: CARLA autonomous vehicle control (carla_bridge --control ros with vehicle_control.py: curvature-to-Ackermann steering, speed PI loop, safety watchdog, and collision referee) and physical rover hardware (stm32_bridge_node: TinyFrames serial, 100 Hz PID).

Detailed Sequential Sub-Steps

Step 11.1
Rate: 20 Hz (sim tick) Frame: base_footprint

Ackermann Curvature Steering (vehicle_control.py)

Converts Twist (vx, wz) into Ackermann front-wheel angle: curvature kappa = wz / vx, steering delta = atan(L * kappa) with wheelbase L=1.525m. Maps to CARLA steer [-1, 1] clamped to max 35° (2.18m min turning radius).

Step 11.2
Rate: 20 Hz Frame: Vehicle Chassis

Speed PI Regulation & Standstill Gear Interlock

A PI controller (Kp=0.35, Ki=0.25) regulates throttle/brake against measured forward speed. Gear transitions (forward/reverse) enforce a complete vehicle standstill before switching to protect powertrain.

Step 11.3
Rate: Continuous (20 Hz) Frame: Watchdog Loop

Safety Watchdog & E-Stop Interlock

Commands full braking if /cmd_vel is older than 0.5s (watchdog timeout) or if /carla/estop is received. Slew limiter caps steering rate to 1.5 units/s and enforces max speed <= 4.0 m/s.

Step 11.4
Rate: Event / 100 Hz Frame: carla_world / Hardware ECU

Collision Sensor Telemetry & Hardware STM32 Mode

In CARLA, /carla/ego/collision records any physical actor contact impulses as a referee for benchmark scenarios. On physical rover hardware, stm32_bridge_node sends 0xA5 0x01 TinyFrames to 4-wheel skid-steer H-bridges.

# Curvature k = w / v floored at v_min_steer
d = math.atan(self.L * w_cmd / v_ref) if w_cmd else 0.0
d = max(-self.max_steer, min(self.max_steer, d))
target_steer = -d / self.max_wheel_steer

# PI Speed control loop
err = target_speed - speed
self.integral = max(-1.0, min(1.0, self.integral + err * dt))
throttle = self.ff0 + self.ff1 * target_speed + self.kp * err + self.ki * self.integral
