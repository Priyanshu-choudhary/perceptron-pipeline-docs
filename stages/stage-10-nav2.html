Stage 10 • Autonomous Navigation & GPS Routing

Nav2 Planning & Motion Control

Full autonomous navigation pipeline to GPS goals. Features gps_goal_node for datum conversion and lane graph routing, Nav2 Smac Hybrid-A* (Dubins car model with 3m turning radius), Regulated Pure Pursuit, nav2_collision_monitor safety zones, and predictive_speed_limit_node for corridor-crossing yielding.

Detailed Sequential Sub-Steps

Step 10.1
Rate: On Goal / 30m Sub-goals Frame: map

GPS-to-Map Datum & Lane-Graph Route Routing

gps_goal_node projects GPS lat/lon to map via origin datum (19.1334° N, 72.9133° E). Finds shortest route across /carla/map/road_graph lane polylines (with lane change edges, +5m cost) and emits 30m sub-goals to NavigateToPose.

Step 10.2
Rate: 2 Hz Replanning Frame: map

Nav2 SmacPlannerHybrid (Dubins Car Kinematics)

Computes kinematically feasible car trajectories with minimum turning radius r = 3.0m on rolling 120m global costmap fed by /costmap/nav. Treats unknown cells as free (0 cost) to eliminate plan timeouts (0.01s vs 0.94s).

Step 10.3
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: odom

Predictive Corridor Yielding (predictive_speed_limit_node)

Checks Stage 7/8 predicted trajectories against the ego corridor. If an object crosses ahead, publishes dynamic speed caps on /speed_limit ensuring the car stops 1.5m short; extends paths until objects clear corridor.

Step 10.4
Rate: 20 Hz Frame: base_footprint

Regulated Pure Pursuit & Nav2 Collision Monitor

Tracks path carrot (1.5-6.0m horizon, 1.2s lookahead) at 2.5 m/s cruise. collision_monitor filters /cmd_vel_nav: halts car on LiDAR points within 1.3m stop polygon, and slows to 40% in 5.0m slow polygon.

# predictive_speed_limit_node.py: Corridor yielding calculation
# Never reach a spot before a crossing person has cleared it, stop 1.5m short
def limit_for_crossing(path_ego, obj_predicted_path, gap=1.5, decel=2.0):
    t_obj_clear, s_corridor = compute_intersection(path_ego, obj_predicted_path)
    if t_obj_clear is not None:
        v_max = (s_corridor - gap) / t_obj_clear
        return min(v_max, math.sqrt(2 * decel * max(0.0, s_corridor - gap)))
    return 4.0 # unconstrained cruise
