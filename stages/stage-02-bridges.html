Stage 02 • Transport & Abstraction

Hardware & Sim Bridges

Decouples ROS 2 graph from transport layers (UART serial, ZeroMQ over LAN, and CARLA RPC socket) with independent sliding-window clock discipline.

Detailed Sequential Sub-Steps

Step 2.1
Rate: Continuous Frame: host_clock

ZeroMQ Sliding-Window Clock Discipline

jetson_bridge_node calculates minimum (t_recv - t_send) over a 3.0s window to eliminate Wi-Fi jitter without running NTP.

Step 2.2
Rate: 50 Hz Frame: base_link

TinyFrames Serial Protocol Decoding

stm32_bridge_node deserializes 0xA5 0x55 telemetry packets (bus voltage, motor currents, encoder ticks) at 115200 baud.

Step 2.3
Rate: 20 Hz Sim Frame: carla_world -> base_footprint

CARLA Synchronous Simulation Bridge

carla_bridge locks UE4 simulation tick, reads synchronized LiDAR, camera, and IMU data, and broadcasts static/dynamic TF frames.
