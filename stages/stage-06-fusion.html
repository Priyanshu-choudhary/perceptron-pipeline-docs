Stage 06 • Multi-Modal Association

Camera-LiDAR Sensor Fusion

Fuses 2D camera bounding boxes (YOLO/reference detector) with 3D LiDAR obstacle points via camera projection and Hungarian affinity matching.

Detailed Sequential Sub-Steps

Step 6.1
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: camera_optical_link

LiDAR-to-Camera Optical Projection

Transforms 3D obstacle clusters from base_footprint to camera_optical_link, applying pinhole projection K and distortion D.

Step 6.2
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: camera_optical_link

Two-Stage Frustum Culling

Discards points behind camera (Z <= 0.1m) and outside normalized optical radius (r > max_radius) to prevent projection wraparound.

Step 6.3
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: base_footprint

IoU & Depth Affinity Association

Computes IoU between projected 3D points and 2D bounding boxes; applies Hungarian matching to associate classes with 3D clusters.

Step 6.4
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: base_footprint

3D Bounding Extent & Range Synthesis

Calculates precise Euclidean distance to nearest point surface and bounds 3D centroid with associated covariance matrix.
