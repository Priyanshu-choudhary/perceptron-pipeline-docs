Sensors & Sources

Physical rover transducers and CARLA UE4 Town10 synthetic equivalents capturing environmental geometry, inertial dynamics, wheel ticks, and imagery.

Detailed Sequential Sub-Steps

Step 1.1
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: lidar3d_link

3D LiDAR Spatial Scanning

Velodyne VLP-16 (16-beam, 30 kHz point rate, 10 Hz rotation, 100 m range, 15° downward pitch) or RPLiDAR D500 2D scanner emitting range/azimuth rays.

Step 1.2
Rate: 50-100 Hz Frame: imu_link

Inertial Measurement (IMU)

Pixhack 2.8.4 internal MPU-6000 6-axis gyroscope and accelerometer capturing body rates (rad/s) and specific forces (+9.81 m/s² z at rest).

Step 1.3
Rate: 20-50 Hz Frame: base_link

Wheel Quadrature Encoders

High-resolution quadrature optical encoders on 4 high-torque geared DC motor shafts measuring rotational pulses per millisecond.

Step 1.4
Rate: 5 Hz Frame: gps_link

Global Navigation Satellite (GNSS)

U-blox NEO-M8N GPS module measuring WGS84 Geodetic latitude, longitude, and ellipsoidal altitude with covariance estimates.

Step 1.5
Rate: 15-30 Hz Frame: camera_optical_link

Monocular Vision Stream

Sony IMX219 / Wide-Angle camera capturing 1280x720 RGB frames with calibrated intrinsic matrix K and distortion coefficients D.

# sim_sensors.py: Realistic IMU attitude random walk + gyro bias
class ImuOrientation:
    def __init__(self, rng, dt, yaw_bias=math.radians(2.0), 
                 yaw_walk_std=math.radians(0.05), yaw_noise_std=math.radians(0.5)):
        self.yaw_offset = yaw_bias
        self.walk_step = yaw_walk_std * math.sqrt(dt)
        self.yaw_std = yaw_noise_std
        
    def measure(self, roll, pitch, yaw):
        self.yaw_offset += self.rng.normal(0.0, self.walk_step)
        return (roll + self.rng.normal(0.0, 0.005),
                pitch + self.rng.normal(0.0, 0.005),
                wrap_angle(yaw + self.yaw_offset + self.rng.normal(0.0, self.yaw_std)))
