Stage 09 • Spatial Risk Modeling

3D-Aware Dynamic Costmap

Synthesizes a rolling 3-layer 2D/3D costmap in odom frame: Layer 1 Voxel Height Obstacles, Layer 2 Inscribed Inflation, and Layer 3 Dynamic Spatio-Temporal Risk Ribbons.

Detailed Sequential Sub-Steps

Step 9.1
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: odom

Layer 1: 3D Voxel Height Obstacle Slicing

Discretizes obstacle points into 5cm height voxels above ground; projects occupied voxels between 0.1m and 1.6m onto 2D occupancy grid.

Step 9.2
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: odom

Layer 2: Inscribed & Exponential Cost Inflation

Buffers lethal obstacles with inscribed rover radius (r=0.33m / 0.75m), decaying outwards via exponential cost scaling: cost = 100 * exp(-s * (dist - r_inscribed)).

Step 9.3
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: odom

Layer 3: Spatio-Temporal Dynamic Risk Ribbons

Rasterizes predicted vehicle/pedestrian paths with Gaussian lateral clearance, weighted inversely by time-to-encounter (decaying from 100 at 0s to 30 at 2s).

Step 9.4
Rate: 10 Hz Frame: odom

Layer 4: Cell Fusion & Maximum Pooling

Combines Layer 2 and Layer 3 via max-pooling: combined_cost(x, y) = max(inflated_cost(x, y), dynamic_risk_cost(x, y)).
